    how it works

    three steps, all on chain.

    1. lockthe organiser puts the round's total and the list's fingerprint on chain before anyone is paid.
    2. payone transaction pays every winner. each amount is hidden, and the contract refuses any payout that doesn't add up to the locked total or the locked headcount.
    3. checkanyone holding the list can confirm it's the one that was locked. no screenshots, no "trust me".

    what this proves: the campaign locked this exact total for this exact list before anyone was paid, and paid exactly that many people. what it doesn't show is who got how much. that stays private, which is the point. one honest limit: in a very small round, shares are easier to guess from the total.
