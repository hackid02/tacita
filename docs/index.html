how a round moves

locked first, paid once, checkable forever.

the organiser locks the total and the list's fingerprint in a campaign contract. one payout sends a hidden output to each winner. anyone holding the list can check it against what was locked.

01lock

tacita create puts the total and the sha-256 fingerprint of the winners list into a new campaign, funded up front.

02pay

tacita pay sends one hidden output per winner. the contract refuses any payout that misses the locked total or headcount.

03check

anyone with the list recomputes the fingerprint, here in the browser or with the cli, and compares.

each hidden output can only be opened by its winner. the contract checks there is exactly one output per winner and that they add up to the locked total, so a round can't go to more or fewer people, or pay out a different total. how the total is split is the organiser's call, and each winner sees their own amount in their wallet.

    the contract

    seven rules, checked on chain every payout.

    1. only the campaign owner can pay
    2. a round can only be paid once
    3. exactly one hidden output per winner, up to 16
    4. no output carries a viewable balance proof
    5. no output promises a minimum value
    6. nothing is paid out in the clear
    7. the amount taken from the vault equals the locked total
